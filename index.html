About me

I'm a GIS professional with more than seven years of experience building, maintaining and automating spatial data systems. My focus now is on GIS development: building the web maps, tools and data pipelines that turn geospatial data into something people can use.

My background runs from imagery to infrastructure. At Wiser Imagery Services, I did feature extraction and remote sensing work for geospatial intelligence clients. Later I joined the R&D Special Projects team, where I wrote Python scripts that ran large-scale data conflation overnight. I also managed PostgreSQL geodatabases and deployed and maintained enterprise SDE environments with ArcGIS Server. Most recently, at Atmos Energy, I worked on utility network data in ArcGIS and Smallworld GDO. I turned as-built projects into accurate pipeline records, managed asset inventory and trained teammates on GIS best practices.

Across both roles, the work I enjoyed most was development. That meant scripting a repetitive workflow away, writing SQL to clean up a messy dataset, or building an interactive map with the ArcGIS Maps SDK for JavaScript. I care about clean data models, maintainable code and applications that make complex spatial information easy to understand.

I hold a B.S. in Geographic Information Systems from Austin Peay State University, and I'm currently completing a Master's in Data Analytics there. My current projects combine GIS and data science, such as a storm-driven power outage risk model for Clarksville, TN.

I'm looking for a GIS Developer role where I can build web mapping applications, automate spatial workflows and help teams get more out of their geospatial data.
